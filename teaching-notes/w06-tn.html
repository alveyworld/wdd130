Week 06 Teacher Notes

Student Expectations At Home

  • Convert portal plan to HTML code (Progress Check 200)
  • Complete Favorite Devotional ICE challenge (if not completed in class)
  • Complete Holiday Spotlight ICE challenge (if not completed in class)
  • Watch a video about frontend development. Take the quiz.

Class Lesson Suggestions

  • Review:
    Review html/css concepts that will be needed to complete the ice challenges.
  • Introduce ICE challenge: Favorite Devo Table
    Demonsrate how to unzip the starter files and save them to the wdd130/ice folder. Explain the expectation of the challenge. Then give students half the class to work together and try to complete the challenge assignment. Visit with students to assess their progress, give feedback, and give them scaffolding as needed.
  • Introduce ICE challenge: Holiday Page
    Demonsrate how to unzip the starter files and save them to the wdd130/ice folder.
    Give students the rules of the challenge
    • No Changing the HTML
    • Make your own unique CSS design (similar to https://csszengarden.com/)
    • Everything goes including AI
    • Holiday site parade for last 5 minutes of class
      (everyone walk around and look at each other's work)
  • Class Discussion: Have a short discussion about being a frontend developer and how HTML and CSS fit with that career

Week 06 Goals

  • Practice creating HTML tables and styling them.
  • Learn the diversity of styling with CSS and build class unity.
  • Practice converting a design to HTML with the portal page.
  • Think about careers for Web Development. Specifically Frontend Development.

Internet Pioneers Exploration

Overview

  • Task: Create a presentation about the people who pioneered the internet and web
  • Purpose: To review the basic terms of the internet and the web through the people who invented them.

Instructions

Choose one of the Pioneers and answer the following questions:

  1. Origin story: Where were they born and where did end up?
  2. Claim to fame: What did they invent? How did they do it?
  3. Latest news: What are they doing today?
  4. Editorial: What is an interesting fact or story about them?

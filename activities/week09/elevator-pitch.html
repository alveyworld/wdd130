Creating an Elevator Pitch

Activity Instructions

  1. Choose a personal site idea

    The Final project for this class begins with designing a website on a topic of your choice. It is time to start planning this personal site. The first step in the planning process is to determine the purpose of the site and what you want to do.

    Then follow the remaining steps to create an elevator pitch for your personal site idea. Creating and sharing an elevator pitch can be a good gauge of the quality of your ideas and your enthusiasm for them.

    So What is an Elevator pitch anyway?

    An elevator pitch is a brief, persuasive speech that you can use to spark interest in what you or your organization does. You can also use one to create interest in a project, idea, or product.

    The 9 C's of elevator pitches (Elevator Pitch Essentials)

    Concise
    An effective elevator pitch contains as few words as possible, but no fewer.
    Clear
    Rather than being filled with acronyms, MBA-speak, and ten-dollar words, an effective elevator pitch can be understood by your grandparents, your spouse, and your children.
    Compelling
    An effective elevator pitch explains the problem your Solution solves.
    Credible
    An effective elevator pitch explains why you are qualified to see the problem and to build your Solution.
    Conceptual
    An effective elevator pitch stays at a fairly high level and does not go into too much unnecessary detail.
    Concrete
    As much as is possible, an effective elevator pitch is also specific and tangible.
    Customized
    An effective elevator pitch addresses the specific interests and concerns of the audience.
    Consistent
    Every version of an effective elevator pitch conveys the same basic message.
    Conversational
    Rather than beginning to close the deal, the goal of an elevator pitch is to just set the hook; to start a conversation, or dialogue, with the audience.

  2. Create your pitch

    Use an AI service like Copilot, ChatGPT, or Gemini to assist in creating your elevator pitch.

    Ask AI to create a 75-150 word pitch for your idea that you would like to turn into a website. Include:

    1. A brief explaination of your idea
    2. A general List of information that will be included on your site.
    3. A description of what makes your site unique.

    Analyze the pitch, then ask and refine your pitch in AI until your are happy with the results

  3. Share (In person Class)

    Present your pitch to a group of at least 2 other students in the class and get their feedback. Be sure to roleplay being in an elevator. After sharing and getting feedback post your pitch and the feedback to the Elevator Pitch assignment.

    Share (Online Class)

    Find someone you know to present your pitch and get some feedback on your idea. Submit your pitch online here in text form to the Elevator Pitch assignment.

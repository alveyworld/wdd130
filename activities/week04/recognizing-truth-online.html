Recognizing Truth Online

Overview

  • Task: Watch and read about recognizing truth and apply it online and to technology.
  • Purpose: To help students learn to recognize truth on the internet and add not be deceived.

Background

Truth is divine and unchanging, but there are many online who will try to deceive you. How can you find truth and how can you recognize lies. How can we keep from being deceived online as technology continues to advance. Here are some videos that may help.

Instructions

  1. Watch tips how to evaluate websites

  2. Read

    Learn how to recognize fake websites to avoid being scammed online.
    BBC: Identify Fake Websites

  3. Ponder

    Study how to recognize truth from Elder Bednar's talk Things as they really are 2.0 Then make connections and think about how this applies to websites and online information.

  4. Prove

    Go to the Recognizing Truth Quiz and respond to what you learned.
